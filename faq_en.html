Frequently Asked Questions

Having trouble? Please check here first.
If this doesn't solve it, you can reach us using the contact form at the bottom.

I requested a gift card but haven't received it.

Gift cards are sent within one week of your exchange request.

You can check the status of a pending request under "Exchange History" on the Exchange screen. Please note that a request may be declined if fraudulent activity is detected.

If more than one week has passed and it still hasn't arrived, please contact us using the form below.

Why can't I exchange for a gift card yet?

To help prevent fraud, gift-card exchanges unlock 3 days after you install the app.

After that, you can request one exchange per week.

I'm switching phones. Can I transfer my data?

Yes. Tap the gear icon (⚙️) at the top right of the Exchange screen to transfer your data.

Please complete the transfer before switching devices.

Reward video ads won't show.

Depending on your connection or ad availability, ads may temporarily fail to load. Please wait a little while and try again somewhere with a good connection.
